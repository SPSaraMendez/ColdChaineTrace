
# Problem Statement

## 1. Context

Cold chain logistics involves the storage, handling and transportation of products that require controlled environmental conditions throughout different stages of their logistical journey.

During these processes, different types of information are generated, including temperature measurements, humidity measurements, timestamps, locations, shipment information and logistics events. The availability, organization and integrity of this information are important for understanding the conditions under which a product has been stored and transported.

ColdChainTrace proposes the development of a technological prototype that combines a physical cold-chain environment with an integrated digital platform for monitoring and traceability.

---

## 2. Current Situation

A cold-chain process can involve multiple stages, including storage, transportation and reception. Throughout these stages, environmental measurements and logistics events may be generated by different devices or systems.

When this information is not integrated into a common traceability mechanism, obtaining a complete historical view of a shipment can become more difficult. It may also be necessary to relate environmental measurements with specific logistics events in order to determine when and under what conditions an incident occurred.

Additionally, the integrity of historical records is an important consideration when information is used to analyze incidents or verify the conditions associated with a shipment.

---

## 3. Problem Definition

The project addresses the technological challenge of designing and developing a modular system capable of integrating environmental monitoring, logistics event management and traceability within a common platform.

The system will be designed to connect:

- Products and lots.
- Shipments.
- Storage and transportation stages.
- Monitoring devices.
- Environmental telemetry.
- Logistics events.
- Alerts and incidents.
- Traceability records.
- Integrity mechanisms for critical information.

To validate the proposed architecture, the project will include a physical cold-chain prototype consisting of a refrigerated enclosure or refrigerator instrumented with an embedded monitoring system.

The physical prototype will acquire environmental data, primarily temperature, and transmit the measurements to the digital platform through an IoT communication mechanism.

The complete system will therefore establish a connection between the physical environment and the digital traceability platform:

Physical environment → Sensor → Embedded system → IoT communication → Backend → Data storage → Traceability → Integrity verification

---

## 4. Consequences

The absence of an integrated monitoring and traceability mechanism can make it more difficult to:

- Obtain a complete historical view of a shipment.
- Relate environmental measurements to logistics events.
- Detect relevant environmental deviations.
- Identify when an environmental incident occurred.
- Associate an incident with the corresponding shipment or monitoring device.
- Analyze the evolution of environmental conditions during a logistics process.
- Verify the integrity of critical historical records.

The project does not assume that existing commercial systems lack these capabilities. Instead, it focuses on exploring how an integrated technological architecture can address these challenges through a controlled engineering prototype.

---

## 5. Technological Opportunity

ColdChainTrace will explore the integration of software engineering, embedded systems, IoT communication, data management and blockchain-based integrity mechanisms to develop a modular prototype for cold-chain monitoring and traceability.

The proposed architecture will contain a generic cold-chain core responsible for common logistics and monitoring operations.

This core will be designed to support different operational modes, initially considering:

- Pharmaceutical mode.
- Food mode.

Each mode may define specific monitoring and validation rules while sharing the same fundamental infrastructure for products, lots, shipments, devices, telemetry, events and traceability.

The project will therefore explore how object-oriented programming, modular architecture and separation of responsibilities can be applied to create a system capable of adapting to different cold-chain scenarios without modifying its fundamental architecture.

---

## 6. Physical Prototype

The project will include the construction and instrumentation of a physical cold-chain prototype within the 21-day development period.

The prototype will consist of a refrigerated enclosure or refrigerator equipped with an embedded monitoring system capable of acquiring environmental measurements.

The embedded system will:

- Acquire temperature measurements.
- Associate measurements with timestamps.
- Process the acquired data.
- Transmit telemetry to the digital platform.
- Allow the generation of controlled environmental scenarios for system validation.

The sensor technology and microcontroller architecture will be selected during the hardware design stage according to technical requirements, availability, accuracy and integration complexity.

The physical prototype will be used as an experimental environment rather than as a certified industrial refrigeration system.

---

## 7. Project Boundary

ColdChainTrace will be developed as an engineering prototype intended to demonstrate the integration between a physical monitoring environment and a digital traceability platform.

The prototype will not attempt to replace certified commercial cold-chain monitoring systems, refrigeration control systems or regulatory compliance platforms.

The project will focus on demonstrating the technological feasibility of:

1. Capturing environmental data from a physical cold-chain environment.
2. Transmitting telemetry through an IoT communication mechanism.
3. Processing and storing the information in a software platform.
4. Associating telemetry with shipments and logistics events.
5. Detecting configured environmental deviations.
6. Generating and managing alerts.
7. Maintaining a traceable history of relevant events.
8. Exploring blockchain-based mechanisms for verifying the integrity of critical records.
9. Supporting different cold-chain operational modes through a modular architecture.
